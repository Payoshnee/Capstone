## Brand & Style

This design system expresses high-tier executive authority, computational precision, and modern enterprise refinement. Inspired by modern deep-space software architectures and refined intelligence suites, the interface pairs deep obsidian matte backdrops with micro-gloss edge reflections, subtle radial lighting flares, and razor-sharp typographic hierarchy. 

The aesthetic is **Matte Gloss Modernism**: ultra-refined, low-noise dark surfaces accented with translucent glass borders, tactile inset rims, and high-energy electric indigo and violet illuminations. Designed for enterprise software suites, executive dashboards, talent intelligence platforms, and critical engineering workflows, it evokes unyielding trust, operational speed, and sophisticated intelligence without visual fatigue.

## Colors

The palette is engineered around pure dark obsidian foundations and disciplined luminous highlights:

- **Foundational Surfaces**: Base backdrop is `#090A0F`, shifting into `#0D0F17` for base canvas containers and `#131622` for standard elevation. Elevated interactive panels sit at `#1E2235` and `#252A40`.
- **Gloss & Structural Glass Lines**: Borders employ a crisp multi-stop translucent rim `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)` to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.03)` with occasional inner highlight strokes of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)`.
- **Primary & Accent Spectrum**: Electric Indigo (`#6366F1`) drives active states and focal calls to action, supported by a secondary Violet tint (`#8B5CF6`) for gradients, badges, and AI intelligence signals.
- **Operational Status Accents**: Emerald (`#10B981`) indicates verified status and success; Cyan (`#06B6D4`) designates operational telemetry; Amber (`#F59E0B`) marks active attention; Crimson (`#EF4444`) reflects blockers or critical security flags.
- **Typographic Neutrals**: Primary text shines in ultra-pure `#FFFFFF` and crisp `#F8FAFC`. Secondary and supporting content rests in calm muted slate (`#94A3B8`), with tertiary labels in `#64748B`.

## Typography

Typographic scale balances geometric modern display power with ultra-legible utilitarian body textures:

- **Display & Headlines**: Plus Jakarta Sans provides refined confidence, tight kerning, and architectural balance. Letter-spacing tightens as the size scales to preserve visual density.
- **Body & Data Grid**: Inter renders dense enterprise information, system telemetry, and narrative copy with optical clarity across all pixel ratios.
- **Micro Overlines & Eyebrows**: Set in `label-sm` with full uppercase and `0.06em` tracking, paired with subtle gradient color fills to cleanly segment content tiers.
- **Code & Numeric Precision**: JetBrains Mono supports tabular financial metrics, verification hashes, and API integration payloads.

## Layout & Spacing

Layout geometry follows a disciplined 12-column responsive fluid grid inside a maximum container width of `1280px` to `1440px`.

- **Breakpoints**: 
  - Desktop: `>= 1024px` (12 columns, 24px gutters, 48px outer margin).
  - Tablet: `768px – 1023px` (8 columns, 20px gutters, 32px outer margin).
  - Mobile: `< 768px` (4 columns, 16px gutters, 20px outer margin).
- **Rhythm & Structure**: Component padding strictly utilizes the 4px/8px modular base scale (`space-xs` through `space-xl`). Sections retain substantial breathing room with vertical section padding ranging from 80px to 120px to let dark matte containers stand out with executive calm.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy leverages **Tonal Layers with Glassmorphic Inset Sheen** rather than murky drop shadows:

- **Tier 0 (Backdrop)**: Matte canvas `#090A0F` with optional ultra-diffused atmospheric radial glows (`rgba(99, 102, 241, 0.08)` blur 120px).
- **Tier 1 (Base Cards & Modules)**: Background `#0D0F17` to `#131622` with a 1px perimeter border of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.07)` and inner top highlight line `inset 0 1px 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.
- **Tier 2 (Elevated Panels & Flyouts)**: Background `#1E2235` blended with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`, perimeter border `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`, and ambient shadow `0 20px 40px -15px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.65)`.
- **Tier 3 (Active Overlays & Modals)**: Background `rgba(30, 34, 53, 0.85)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(24px)`, crisp gloss edges, and high-spread shadow `0 30px 60px -12px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.8)`.

## Shapes

The design system implements a consistent **Rounded** radius token architecture (`roundedness: 2`):
- Standard micro-elements, inputs, and chips leverage `8px` (`0.5rem`).
- Cards, data modules, and modal containers utilize `16px` (`1rem`) to establish a soft-touch executive feel.
- Hero blocks and large featured panels employ `24px` (`1.5rem`).
- Pills and operational badges use full pill rounding (`9999px`) to immediately separate metadata tags from rectangular content containers.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action**: Electric indigo fill (`#6366F1`) shifting to violet tint (`#8B5CF6`) via a 135-degree gradient, crisp white text, `8px` corner radius, with subtle glossy top inner edge highlight `inset 0 1px 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.25)` and faint indigo drop glow `0 8px 20px -4px rgba(99, 102, 241, 0.45)`.
- **Secondary / Glass Button**: Translucent background `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.04)`, 1px outline of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.10)`, text `#F8FAFC`. On hover: surface transitions to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)` and border becomes `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)`.
- **Tertiary / Ghost**: Transparent surface, muted slate text (`#94A3B8`), subtle hover transition to text `#FFFFFF` with faint `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)` background.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Inputs**: Background `#0D0F17`, 1px border `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`, text `#F8FAFC`, placeholder `#64748B`, `8px` radius. Focus shifts border to `#6366F1` with an ambient glow `0 0 0 3px rgba(99, 102, 241, 0.18)`.
- **Checkboxes & Radios**: Custom matte slate box with `4px` radius (round for radio), 1px border `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)`. Active state triggers `#6366F1` background with a crisp white micro-check SVG.

### Cards & Modular Containers
- **Content Panels**: Obsidian background `#131622`, 1px border `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.07)`, `16px` radius. Interactive hover adds a soft gloss sheen across the top-right corner with a subtle border transition to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)`.
- **Stat & Metric Cards**: Features an uppercase `label-sm` title, oversized Plus Jakarta Sans stat value in `#FFFFFF`, and an inline status chip reflecting velocity delta.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status Chips**: `9999px` pill radius, padding `4px 10px`, typography `label-sm`.
  - **Success / Verified**: Background `rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.12)`, border `rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.25)`, text `#34D399`.
  - **Intelligence / Active**: Background `rgba(99, 102, 241, 0.12)`, border `rgba(99, 102, 241, 0.25)`, text `#A5B4FC`.
  - **Neutral**: Background `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)`, border `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`, text `#94A3B8`.

### Lists & Data Tables
- **Lists**: Clean horizontal dividers using `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)`. Hovering rows reveals a light satin tint of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.02)`.
- **Header Cells**: Typography `label-sm`, text `#64748B`, uppercase tracking `0.05em`.
